Keyword extraction

Rank the most informative terms and phrases. Compare TF-IDF, RAKE and YAKE on the same input.

Scores are normalised to 0–1 for display. English stop words are removed.

Text summarisation

Extractive mode selects original sentences. Abstractive mode generates new wording with a pretrained model.

Abstractive summarisation loads a Hugging Face model on first use (about 1.2 GB) and can take 30–90 seconds on CPU. It requires at least 30 words.

Compression is computed from actual word counts, not estimated.

Language detection

Identify the dominant language with a Naive Bayes character n-gram classifier.

Short or mixed-language text produces lower confidence. Coverage is limited to langdetect's bundled profiles.

Sentiment analysis

Score positive, neutral and negative language with VADER's lexicon and rules.

VADER is tuned for English. Scores describe language polarity, not a person's true emotion.
